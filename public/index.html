How to play

  • WASD move · Shift sprint · Mouse aim · LMB fire
  • 1-9 / Shift+wheel weapons · Wheel zoom
  • E board any vehicle you're standing next to: your own (its AI crew hops out), an EMPTY one anywhere (even an enemy's) or a wild horse. AI pilots and drivers crew every other vehicle and re-board empty ones.
  • Flying: jets roll down an airport runway (hold W or press L) to take off, turn with momentum, and land with L when lined up over a runway or carrier deck. Helicopters lift off / land anywhere with L. Shift afterburner · EE eject · RMB homing missiles lock onto the enemy nearest your cursor.
  • Driving: tanks and ships accelerate, turn and brake realistically; steer against your heading to reverse. Tank turrets follow the mouse.
  • Every kingdom has an airport and a fenced military base. Abandoned airfields hide empty jets you can steal!
  • Free towns dot the map. Order troops to Attack a town hall and hold it with no defenders for 6 s to capture it.
  • Wolves, bears and sharks hunt; deer, rabbits and eagles flee. Aliens invade every few minutes!
  • Command mode Q (or hold Ctrl): click a unit or drag a box to select. Right-click = smart order: move, attack an enemy, or board an EMPTY vehicle. The unit card has Talk, Follow, Hold, Move, Attack, Board, Get out (G), Take off and Land (L).
  • Pilots fly any aircraft, soldiers drive tanks, trucks and boats. Tell a selected pilot "take the helicopter and attack" or "land and get out": they remember your conversation.
  • Z X C V orders go to the selected units (or the whole army if none; Alt = nearby only) · T talk to the selected unit · L in a jet = autopilot landing at the nearest runway
  • Joint HQ J: propose alliances (allies never shoot each other and share hospitals), then launch joint operations: Strike, Defense, Capture Town or Air Patrol. Forces from every allied kingdom gather at a staging point, then attack together: armour first, infantry behind, artillery from range, aircraft overhead. Set how much of your army joins your allies' operations.
  • Real worlds: pick India or Earth in the lobby for real coastlines, mountains, deserts and rivers, with kingdoms at real capitals and real cities as towns. Indian towns have streets, shops, temples, traffic and trains; Himalayan villages have monasteries and prayer flags.
  • Missiles: jets carry missiles on their pylons (you can see them). Hold the cursor over an enemy until the diamond turns red (LOCK) and press RMB: aircraft get air-to-air missiles, ground targets get air-to-ground missiles automatically. Pylons reload slowly in flight and fully once you land. Space drops flares that can decoy missiles chasing you; AI pilots use them too.
  • Traffic: autos, taxis, buses, scooters and lorries drive on the left. Press E next to one to grab it (the driver runs off). Ambulances and army supply trucks heal troops around them.
  • Indian arsenal: HAL AMCA (stealth, hard to spot), Su-30MKI and BrahMos launchers, BrahMos batteries and Kolkata-class destroyers fire the long-range BrahMos cruise missile. Also Para SF commandos, snipers, K9 Vajra, Pinaka, BMP-2, INS Vikrant.
  • Click your kingdom name (top-left) or use Joint HQ to rename your kingdom and towns. 🏠 Home (top-right) returns to the lobby; your kingdom is saved. The 📚 Gallery shows every unit and building.
  • B build · U raise units · F forge anything with AI · Enter chat · M music
  • Day turns to night every 10 minutes: windows glow, torches flicker, and rain rolls in from time to time.
